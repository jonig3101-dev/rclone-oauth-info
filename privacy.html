Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

1. Purpose

Rclone Google Drive is a personal application used to connect my own Google Drive account to rclone on Fedora Linux.

2. Google User Data

The application requests access to Google Drive files and metadata to allow me to view, download, upload, organize, modify, and delete files through rclone.

3. Data Storage and Sharing

OAuth credentials and access tokens are stored locally in my rclone configuration. Files may be cached locally during normal operation.

The application does not operate a separate server for collecting or storing Google Drive user data. Google Drive data is not sold or shared with advertisers or third parties by this application.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally cached files are managed by rclone. OAuth credentials can be removed by deleting the corresponding rclone configuration or revoking access in Google Account settings.

5. Security

Google OAuth 2.0 is used for authentication. Credentials are not published on this website. Access to local configuration files is restricted to the device owner.

6. Contact

For questions about this personal application, contact the developer through the associated Google OAuth consent screen.
